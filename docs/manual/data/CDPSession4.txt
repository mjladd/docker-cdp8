CDPSession4.txt - Worksheet for CDP Sound Design 2020 Session 4 04 Dec 2019

General topic:  'Mingling' Sounds

Source sounds needed:  spr1.wav, count.wav, marimba.wav, stoneflg.wav


			A - In the TIME DOMAIN

1) PREPARE a sound with TIMESTRETCH and LOOP (Save all these files)
1a)  PVOC ANAL 1 spr1.wav spr1.ana
1b)  STRETCH TIME spr1.ana => spr1x3.ana 3
1c)  PVOC SYNTH spr1x3.ana spr1x3.wav
1d)  EXTEND LOOP spr1x3.wav (looplen: 60ms, advance_step: 30ms, splice: 25ms )
     => spr1x3loop.wav (Note the attack portion;  it's too long, so:)
1e)  REVERSE spr1x3loop.wav spr1x3loopR.wav (-> loud part at end)
1f)  ENVEL DOVETAIL spr1x3loopR.wav spr1x3loopRdt.wav 0 0.5 0 0 (tidy end)

2) MINGLING WITH AMPLITUDE ENVELOPE
2a) CROSSOVER:  Crossfade from 1st soundfile to 2nd
     SUBMIX CROSSFADE 1 count.wav spr1x3loopRdt.wav counttospr.wav 
     stagger 0, crossfade start 0, crossfade end 16.13 (end of file 2)

2b) TRANSFER: count.wav's envelope onto spr1x3loopRdt.wav 
     ENVEL REPLACE 1 spr1x3loopRdt.wav count.wav spr1replacecnt.wav 10
     PVOC ANAL 1 spr1replacecnt.wav spr1replacecnt.ana (Save to use later)
     

			B - In the SPECTRAL DOMAIN
			
1) PREPARE analysis files: start with spr1.wav and count.wav (Save)
     PVOC ANAL 1 spr1x3loopRdt.wav => spr1x3loopRdt.ana 
     PVOC ANAL 1 count.wav => count.ana

2) REPLACE SPECTRAL ENVELOPE OF 1ST FILE WITH THAT OF 2ND (timbre!)
   (2nd sound's envelope gradually onto the 1st sound, so put the first 
    sound into CHOSEN FILES first)
   (interp is degree of replacement Range: 0 to 1)
     COMBINE CROSS count.ana spr1x3loopRdt.ana cntcrossspr.ana 0.98

3) VOCODING (Impose spectral envelope of 2nd sound onto the 1st sound)

     (Spring envelope onto voice's: voice speaks with sound of the spring, 
      so count is first in CHOSEN FILES and spring is second)
3a)  FORMANTS VOCODE count.ana spr1x3loopRdt.ana sprcnt1f.ana -f10
3b)  FORMANTS VOCODE count.ana spr1x3loopRdt.ana sprcnt1p.ana -p6

     (Count envelope onto spring's: spring speaks with sound of the count, 
      so reverse the order of the files in CHOSEN FILES: 1st spring then 
      count)
3c)  FORMANTS VOCODE spr1x3loopRdt.ana count.ana cntspr1f.ana -f15
3d)  FORMANTS VOCODE spr1x3loopRdt.ana count.ana cntspr1p.ana -p6 -g0.8
     (May need to re-do with -g0.8 if overloads)
 
4) INTERLEAVE TWO ANALYSIS FILES
     COMBINE INTERLEAVE count.ana spr1x3loopRdt.ana cntinterlspr.ana 3
     COMBINE INTERLEAVE spr1x3loopRdt.ana count.ana sprinterlcnt.ana 3
   
5) MAX WINDOW RETAINED
     COMBINE MAX count.ana spr1x3loopRdt.ana cntsprmax.ana

6) BRIDGE (transition between two analysis windows, time 0.1 in first 
     sound and time 7.5 in the second sound)
6a)  (In Mode 1, the transition is the result of direct interpolation)
     MORPH BRIDGE 1 spr1x3loopRdt.ana count.ana sprbridgecntm1.ana -f0.1 
     -g7.5
6b) (In mode 2, the transition follows the minimum amplitudes)
    (amp & frq start: 0; amp & frq end: 1; begin at time 0.1 end at time 7.5)
     MORPH BRIDGE 2 spr1x3loopRdt.ana count.ana sprbridgecntm2.ana -f0.1 -g7.5
     (There are other modes to explore)

7) GLIDE (transition between two single time-specified analysis windows - 
     note the duration parameter (in seconds)!)
     (SPEC GRAB in Sound Loom is Process > SIMPLE > grab window)
7a)  SPEC GRAB spr1x3loopRdt.ana grabspr.ana 9.1 
     SPEC GRAB count.ana grabcnt.ana 0.3
     MORPH GLIDE grabspr.ana grabcnt.ana sprtocnt.ana 30
     
8) MORPH between sounds
8a) Preparations (done earlier):
    1. NB: using reversed spring sound so is stronger at the end 
    (spr1x3loopRdt.ana made earlier)
    2. Impose the amplitude envelope of the count onto that of the spring and 
    convert to an analysis file (done in 2b above: spr1replacecnt.ana)
    
8b) Direct morph without a middle step (start & end amp & frq times the same, 
    exponents are 1 for no curve)
    MORPH MORPH 1 count.ana spr1x3loopRdt.ana cntmorphtospr1.ana 0.5 8.0 0.5 8.0 1 1
    
8c) Morph using spring enveloped with count's amplitude envelope:
    MORPH MORPH 1 count.ana spr1replacecnt.ana cntmorphtospr2.ana 0.5 8.0 0.5 8.0 1 1
    Note:  this works better but we hear the enveloping right to the end.
    
8d) Thus the logic of morphing in 3 stages as in the Course Notes:
    1) morph count to enveloped spring = m1
       MORPH MORPH 1 count.ana spr1replacecnt.ana m1.ana 0.5 8.0 0.5 8.0 1 1
    2) morph enveloped spring to reversed not-enveloped spring = m2
       MORPH MORPH 1 spr1replacecnt.ana spr1x3loopRdt.ana m2.ana 0.5 8.0 0.5 8.0 1 1
    3) morph from count to morph of enveloped spring to reversed 
       not-enveloped spring: m1 to m2 = m3
       MORPH MORPH 1 m1.ana m2.ana m3.ana 0.5 10.0 0.5 10.0 1 1
       
    Adjusting timings and amp frq weightings can improve the result.  There will be other ways to develop a middle step to get a sound with features from both 'end' sounds.

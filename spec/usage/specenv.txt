USAGE:
specenv specenv inanalfil1 inanalfil2 outanalfil windowsize [-bbal] [-p] [-i] [-k]

Get the spectral envelope of file 2 and apply it to file 1.
(File 2 must be at least as long as file 1).

BAL  proportion of original files in output.
     bal > 1 : balance of shaped-file to infile1 = (1-bal) : bal
               (Higher balance keeps more of the original file1)
     bal < 1 : balance of shaped-file to infile2 = (1+bal) : -bal
               (Lower balance keeps more of the imposed file2)
     default 0.0 (none of the original files in the output).

-p : windowsize counted in octave steps.
     Default: counted in analysis-channel-widths (srate/anal-chans).

-i : IMPOSE spectral envelope (default, REPLACE spectral envelope)

-k : KEEP the loudness contour of the imposed file.
     Default: keep the loudness contour of the original file.

Process equivalent to format extraction + formant impose/replace
but allows for larger spectral windows.

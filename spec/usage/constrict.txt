USAGE:
constrict constrict infile outfile constriction

Shorten duration of any zero-levels sections in sound.

CONSTRICTION is the percentage deletion of zero-level areas.
  Values 0 - 100 shorten zero-sections by that percentage.
  Values > 100 (i.e. shrinking silence by more than 100%)
  cause sound on either side of silence to be overlapped
  by "constriction - 100.0".
  Max value 200.

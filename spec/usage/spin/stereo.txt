USAGE:  spin stereo
1 inf outf rate dopl xbuf [-bboost] [-aatten] [-eexpbuf]
2 inf outf rate chns cntr dopl xbuf [-bboost] [-aatt] [-kcmn] [-ccmx]
3 inf outf rate chns cntr dopl xbuf [-bboost] [-aatt] [-kcmn]

Spin a wide stereo-image across the stereo space.
(with possible doppler-shift on the moving edges).

MODES 2 & 3 create 3-chan-wide output, centred on channel "centre",
       in an "ochans"-channel outfile.

When the spinning image crosses the centre ....
MODE 2 uses outer channels to project stereo-at-centre image.
MODE 3 uses ONLY central channel to project stereo-at-centre image.

RATE  spin speed in cycles per second (can vary over time).
      Positive values spin clockwise (as viewed from above).
      Negative values spin anticlockwise (as viewed from above).
BOOST Multiplicative level changes, as edges pass through centre.
      gradually increase (*boost) as edge passes "FRONT" centre
      and decreases (*1/boost) as edge passes "REAR" centre.
      The two edges pass through centre simultaneously
      so one edge gets louder and the other quieter.
ATT   overall level attenuation (*att) as BOTH edges pass thro centre.
DOPL  Max doppler pitchshift, in semitones (Range 0-12).
XBUF  Expand buffers by this factor (may be ness for large doppler shift)

Mode 2 & 3 only...
CHNS  Number of channels in output file.
CNTR  Output channel which carries the central channel of the output.
CMN   Min level on centre lspkr (0-1).
Mode 2 only...
CMX   Max level on centre lspkr (0-1).

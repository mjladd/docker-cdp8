USAGE: spin quad 1 inf1 inf2 outf
rate ochns cntr dopl xbuf [-bboost] [-aatt] [-kcmn] [-ccmx]
OR:    spin quad 2 inf1 inf2 outf
rate ochns cntr dopl xbuf [-bboost] [-aatt] [-kcmn]

Spin two wide stereo-image across a 5-channel-wide sound image
(with possible doppler pitch-shift of the moving edges).

When the spinning image crosses the centre ....
MODE 1 uses outer channels to project stereo-at-centre image.
MODE 2 uses ONLY central channel to project stereo-at-centre image.

RATE  spin speed in cycles per second (can vary over time).
      Positive values spin clockwise (as viewed from above).
      Negative values spin anticlockwise (as viewed from above).
OCHNS Number of channels in output file.
CNTR  Output channel which carries the central channel of the 5 outputs.
DOPL  Max doppler pitchshift, in semitones (Range 0-12).
XBUF  Expand buffers used by process (may be nesss for large doppler shift)
BOOST Multiplicative level changes, as edges pass through centre.
      gradually increase (*boost) as edge passes "FRONT" centre
      and decreases (*1/boost) as edge passes "REAR" centre.
      The two edges pass through centre simultaneously
      so one edge gets louder and the other quieter.
ATT   overall level decrease (*atten) as BOTH edges pass thro centre.
CMN   Min level on centre lspkr (0-1).
Mode 2 only...
CMX   Max level on centre lspkr (0-1).

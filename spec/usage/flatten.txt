USAGE:
flatten flatten infile outfile elementsize shoulder [-ttail]

Equalise level of sound elements in mono src.

ELEMENTSIZE Approx size of elements (e.g. syllables) in src.
            (Range 0.001 to 100).
SHOULDER    Risetime in segment to changed level (mS).
            (Range 20 to ELEMENTSIZE/2).
            Will never be longer than distance from seg edge to peak.
TAIL        Portion of end of sound to be treated as a whole segment.
            (Range 0 to < duration).

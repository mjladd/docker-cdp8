CDP Release 5 2005
USAGE:	dshift [-dN] infile outfile [distance between speakers]

Calculates Doppler shift for a source moving between two speakers
that are 10m apart and a listener that is at rest,
5m from the line between them.
infile is the pan breakpoint file, as used with modify:pan
outfile is breakpoint file of semitone shifts
to give to modify:speed, Mode 2(semitones).
(NB: modify:pan range: -1 = full left, 1 = full right)
-d is a flag setting the time it takes a sound to change direction.
N is the time in milliseconds. Default: 10ms
This is useful in order to avoid clicks.

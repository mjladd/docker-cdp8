CDP Release 7.1 2016
CONVOLVE THE FIRST SOUND WITH THE SECOND

USAGE: modify convolve 1 infile1 infile2 outfile
OR:    modify convolve 2 infile1 infile2 outfile tranposfile

TRANSPOSFILE is textfile of time / semitone-transposition pairs
infile2 must NOT be longer than infile1
and both files must have the same channel count.

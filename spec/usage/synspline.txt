USAGE: synspline synspline outfile srate dur frq splinecnt interpval seed
       [-smaxspline] [-imaxinterp] [-dpdrift -vdriftrate] [-n]

Synthesis from waveforms made by smoothly joining randomly generated points.

SRATE     Sample rate of synthesized sound.
DUR       Duration of synthesized sound.
FRQ       Fundamental frq of output (Range 0.001 to 10000 Hz) (PTR).
SPLINECNT No. of rand vals (to smooth between) per half-wavecycle (0-64) (PTR).
INTERPVAL No. of wavecycles over which 1 waveshape morphs to next (0 - 4096) (PTR).
SEED      Same seed value produces identical output with same parameters.
MAXSPLINE Max splinecnt: (PTR) Causes random vals of "splinecnt" to be generated,
          in range "SPLINECNT" to "MAXSPLINE" (Ignored if set to zero).
MAXINTERP Max interpval: (PTR) Causes random vals of "interpval" to be generated,
          in range "INTERPVAL" to "MAXINTERP" (Ignored if set to zero).
PDRIFT    Semitone (half)range of random drift in frequency. (0 - 12).
DRIFTRATE Average time (mS) to next drift offset (True time 1/2 to 3/2 of this) (1-1000).
-n        Normalise every cycle (default: cycles retain random valued amplitudes).

(PTR = possibly time-varying).

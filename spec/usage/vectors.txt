USAGE: vectors column1 column2 [outfile] -flag [-eERROR]

	columnN  textfile containing list of numbers.
	outfile  receives output as text (Default: screen)
	flag     determines operation.
	         use 'vectors -h' to see flags.
	-e       ERROR is acceptable error in checking equality of numbers.
	         (Default: 0.000002).

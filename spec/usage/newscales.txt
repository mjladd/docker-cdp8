USAGE: newscales outfile datafile spectrumfile [srate]
outfile      Generic name for output soundfiles (must not end with a number)
datafile     Frq-amp pairs defining note-events in output
spectrumfile Harmonicnumber-amplitude pairs, define spectrum of note.
             One line for all notes
             OR
             One line for each note in the frq-list.
            (First line corresponds to lowest note, etc.)

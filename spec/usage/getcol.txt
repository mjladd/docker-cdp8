USAGE : getcol infile outfile colno [skiplines] [-e]

skiplines  Ignores this number of lines at start of file.
-e         Ignores lines with a single 'e' or 's' (as in CSound scores)
           and comment lines starting with ';'
           (skipped lines should still be counted).

USAGE: specfold specfold 1 inanalfile outanalfile stt len cnt  [-a]
USAGE: specfold specfold 2 inanalfile outanalfile stt len      [-a]
USAGE: specfold specfold 3 inanalfile outanalfile stt len seed [-a]

MODE 1 : Fold the spectrum.

Channels in fold group are split into 2 equal sets of consective channels.
Values in first set are moved from channel N to channel 2N
Values in 2nd set are assigned to remaining channels, in descending order.

MODE 2 : Invert the spectrum.
MODE 2 : Randomise the spectrum.

STT   Lowest spectrum channel to process.
LEN   Number of channels to process (>= 4)
      For Folding (Mode 1) this must be an even number.
CNT   (Mode 1) Number of folds (less than "len").
SEED  (Mode 3) Seed value sets a specific random perm of channels.
-a    Process the amplitudes only.

USAGE:
speclean clean sigfile noisfile outanalfile persist noisgain

Eliminate frqbands in 'sigfile' falling below maxlevels found in 'noisfile'

PERSIST    min time channel signal must exceed noise-level, to be retained.
NOISGAIN   multiplies noiselevels found in noisfile before they are used
           for comparison with infile signal: (Default 2).

Both input files are analysis files.
'noisfil' is a sample of noise (only) from the 'sigfile'.

USAGE:
cantor set infile outfile 1-2 holesize holedig depth-trig splicelen maxdur [-e]
cantor set infile outfile 3   holelev  holedig layercnt layerdec maxdur

Gradually cut hole in central 3rd of input sound.
Cut holes in central 3rd of the remaining segments, and so on.
Output is a sequence of sounds with more and more holes cut.
Mode 3 uses superimposed vibrati envelopes

MODES 1 and 2

HOLESIZE   MODE 1: Percentage of current segment-time taken up by hole.
           Size of hole depends on size of segment being cut.
           MODE 2: (Fixed) Duration of holes.
HOLEDIG    Depth of each cut as hole is gradually created (>0-1).
DEPTH-TRIG Level-Depth of hole triggering next hole-cutting.
SPLICELEN  Splicelength in mS.
MAXDUR     Maximum total duration of all the output sound.
-e         Extend sound beyond splicelen limits.

MODES 3

HOLELEV    Level of signal at base of holes.
HOLEDIG    How many repets before full-depth reached.
LAYERCNT   Number of vibrato layers used.
LAYERDEC   Depth of next vibrato in relation to previous
MAXDUR     Maximum total duration of all the output sound.

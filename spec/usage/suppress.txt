USAGE: suppress partials inanal outanal timeslots lofrq hifrq chancnt

SUPPRESS THE MOST PROMINENT PARTIALS IN THE FRQ BAND INDICATED

TIMESLOTS File with time-pairs between which bands are suppressed.
LOFRQ     Low frequency limit of band where partials to be suppressed.
HIFRQ     High frequency limit of band where partials to be suppressed.
CHANCNT   Number of most prominent channels in band, to be suppressed.

USAGE:
packet packet 1 infile outfile times mindur narrowing centring [-n|-f] [-s]
packet packet 2 infile outfile times dur    narrowing centring [-n|-f] [-s]

Isolate or generate  a sound packet.

Mode 1:    Found packet:  Looks for signal minima as edges of wave-packet.
Mode 2:   Forced packet: Creates packet at specified time.

TIMES     Time (or textfile of times) at which packet is extracted/created.

MINDUR    Min duration of (found) packet (in mS) (less than half src duration).
DUR       Duration of (forced) packet (in mS)  (less than half src duration).

NARROWING Narrowing of packet envelope (0 - 1000).
          Values below 1.0 broaden the packet.
          Values very close to zero may produce clicks (square-wave envelope).
          Very high vals with very short packets may produce
          click-impulses or silence.

CENTRING  Centring of peak of packet envelope.
          0  peak at centre: -1 peak at start: 1 peak at end.
          If packet content has varying level, true peak position may not
          correspond to envelope peak position, unless "-f" flag used.

-n        Normalise packet level.

-f        Packet wave maxima and minima forced up(down)to packet contour.
          (default, packet envelope simply imposed on existing signal).
          (normalisatiojn flag ignored if "-f" flag used

-s        Shave off leading or trailing silence.

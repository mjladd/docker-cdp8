USAGE:
verges verges inf outf times [-ttransp] [-eexp] [-ddur] [-n] [-b|-s]

Play source, with specified brief moments glissing up or down.

TIMES  Textfile listing times of verge-attacks.

TRANS  Semitone transposition at start of verge. (Defuault 5).
EXP    slope of glissi (1(dflt) to 8) : higher vals gliss faster.
DUR    duration of glissi, in mS. (default 100)

-n     Use times given (default looks for peak closest to time in src).
-b     Boost the level of the verges.
-s     Suppress all input except verges. (Boost verges)

"TRANSP", "EXP" and "DUR" can vary through time.

USAGE:
hover hover infile outfile frq loc frqrand locrand splice dur

move through a file, zigzag reading it at a given frequency.

FRQ      rate of reading source-samples (in Hz).
         determines samplewidth of zigzag-read. e.g. at srate 44100,
         frq 1 Hz, reads 22050 samps forward, & 22050 samps back.
         frq 10 Hz, reads 2205 samps forward, & 2205 samps back.
LOC      Time in sourcefile from which samples are read.
FRQRAND  Random variation of frequency, (0-1).
LOCRAND  Random variation of location, (0-1).
SPLICE   length of splices at zig (zag) ends (in mS).
DUR      total output duration.

frq and loc (frqrand and locrand) may vary through time.
time in any brkpoint files is time in output-file.
splicelen must be less than 1 over twice the max frq used.

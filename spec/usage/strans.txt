CHANGE SPEED & PITCH OF (MULTICHANNEL) SRC SOUND, OR ADD VIBRATO.

USAGE: strans multi 1 infile outfile     speed             [-o]
OR:    strans multi 2 infile outfile     semitone-transpos [-o]
OR:    strans multi 3 infile outfile accel goaltime [-sstarttime]
OR:    strans multi 4 infile outfile vibfrq vibdepth

SPEED             is a speed-multiplier
SEMITONE-TRANSPOS is transposition in semitones
    -o  brkpnt times read as outfile times (default: as infile times).

ACCEL     (a speed multiplier) is the speed reached at the goaltime.
GOALTIME  is the time at which the full acceleration is achieved.
STARTTIME is the time at which acceleration begins (default 0.0).

VIBFRQ    is the vibrato frequency in Hz (can vary over time).
VIBDEPTH  is pitch-depth of vibrato (in semitones) (can vary over time).

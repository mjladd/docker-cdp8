USAGE:
sfecho echo infile outfile delay attenuation totaldur [-rrand] [-ccutoff]

DELAY        Time between echo repeats.
ATTENUATION  Relative level of each repeat (0 - 1).
TOTALDUR     Maximum output duration (duration may be less).
RAND         Randomisation of echo times.
CUTOFF       dB Level at which decaying echoes cut off (default -96dB).

Delay, attenuation and randomisation may vary over time.
Delay cannot be less than duration.
 (use "MODIFY REVECHO DELAY" process if overlapping delays required)

USAGE: spectrum fixed outanalfile datafile pointcnt srate dur
                [-hH] [-bB] [-fF] [-rR] [-sS] [-aA] [-tT] [-wW] [-mM] [-d]
Generate fixed spectrum, from input data.

DATAFILE  textfile as "frq amp" pairs
          showing peaks, troughs and amplitude envelope.
          Local peaks indicated by peak frq and amplitude.
          Local troughs indicated by -(frq) and amplitude.
          Elsewhere frq = zero (i.e. unspecific frq within channel)
                    amp = amplitude at channel centre frq.
POINTCNT  Number of analysis points per window.
SRATE     Samplerate of sound which would be generated from anal output data.
DURATION  Duration of sound output.
H    Number of HARMONICS of peaks to be emphasized.
B    BRIGHTNESS: relative loudness of successive harmonics.
     (For TYPES 1,4 & 5 - see below - this is max brightness applied).
F    FRACTION of analchans whose level falls below amp contour of spectrum.
R    Max RANDOM attenuation excursion of amplitude in those channels.
S    Freq SPREAD of peaks (0.0001 to 0.1)
A    ATTENUATION applied to the spectrum.
T    TYPE of spectrum:
   0 Brightness as specified.
   1 Brightness depend on peakwidth.
   2 Peak freq randonly varies (with time) across peak-width.
   3 Ditto, plus harmonics randvary independently of peak frq.
   4 Case 1 + Case 2
   5 Case 1 + Case 3
Following data only useful if t flag > 0
W    WIDTH of peaks (as aspect ratio): zero means no peak info 
     otherwise expects a brkpoint file of pairs of
     peak-frq and peak-aspect (pkheight/pkwidth/nyquist)
M    MAXIMUM peakwidth aspect (corresponding to max brightness)

-d   Minimal dovetailing of output sound.

USAGE:
fofex construct 1 fofbank outf fofinf pbrk env gain f1
fofex construct 2-5 fofbank outf fofinf pbrk env gain [-n]
fofex construct 6
     fofbank outf fofinf pbrk env gain f1 f2 minf maxf [-n]
fofex construct 7
     fofbank outf fofinf pbrk env gain f1 f2 f3 minf maxk minl maxl [-n]

MODES: 1  Use 1 FOF only.
       2  All FOFs superimposed to make output FOF.
       3  Low FOFs superimposed to make output FOF.
       4  Midrange FOFs superimposed to make output FOF.
       5  High FOFs superimposed to make output FOF.
       6  Use 2 FOFs,varying weighting with pitch of output.
       7  Use 3 FOFs,vary weighting with pitch & level of output.

FOFBANK    Soundfile of extracted FOFS created by 'fofex extract'.
PBRK       Brkpnt file of time/frq info. May contain zeros (no-signal)
           but NOT pitch-zeros (no-pitch). Must have SOME frq info.
ENV        Brkpoint-Envelope file, as text.
FOFINF     A textfile with position of pitchsteps in soundfile,
           also created by the 'fofex extract' process.
GAIN       Output gain.
F1(2,3)    In mode 1, FOF to use, (lowest=1).n           In modes 6,7 use specifically numbered FOFs.
MINF MAXF  Min and max freqs for variation of internal FOF balance.
MINL MAXL  Min and max levels(0-1) for variation internal FOF balance.
-n    Set all FOFs to same level before synthesis.

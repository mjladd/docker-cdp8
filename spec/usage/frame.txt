USAGE:
frame shift 1 infile outfile snake rotation [-ssmear]
frame shift 2 infile outfile snake rotation1 rotation2 [-ssmear]
frame shift 3 infile outfile reorient
frame shift 4 infile outfile mirrorplane
frame shift 5 infile outfile [-b]
frame shift 6 infile outfile swapA swapB
frame shift 7 infile outfile chaninfo gain
frame shift 8 infile outfile [-b]

Modes 1 & 2 Rotate the entire frame of a multichannel file.
Mode 3 Changes the channel assignment of a multichannel file.
Mode 4 Mirrors the channel output around specified mirrorplane.
Mode 5 Converts between ring-numbered & bilaterally numbered outchans.
Mode 6 Swaps any pair of channels (swapA and swapB).
Mode 7 Allows any channel, or set of channels, to be enveloped,
       independently of the other channels.
Mode 8 Converts between ring-numbered & BEAST bilateral numbering.

ROTATION    rotation-rate in cycles (complete frame-rotations) per sec.
            Mode 2 (only with files with even number of input channels)
            rotates odd & even chans independently; has 2 rotation vals.
            Negative values produce anticlockwise rotation.
            Rotation rate can vary through time. Range -500 to +500.
SMEAR       Extent to which channel-signals bleed onto adjacent chans.
            (range 0 to 0.5 : default 0)
SNAKE       In clockwise rotation,in (e.g.) 8 chans, input chan-1 follows
            the route 1->2->3->4->5->6->7->8->1 etc. around lspkrs.
            Other input chans form a "snake" following chan-1 round.
            To produce rotation (default) set '"snake" to ZERO.
            HOWEVER, channels might snake along a different route,
            & route (which could vary over time) can be specified
            in a data file of "time : snake-list" values
            e.g. might specify route at time zero ,for 8-chan file
            "0.0 8 6 5 2 7 3 4 1" & change route at a later time
            e.g. "1.378245 4 1 5 2 6 7 8 3"
            1st time-val in data must be zero. Times must increase.
            IN MODE 2, odd & even chans rotate independently.
            1->3->5->7->1 etc and 2->4->6->8->2 etc
            Snake data directs motion round a different route.
            e.g.  the snake "8 6 5 2 7 3 4 1" above
            produces one snake around the odd entries
            i.e. 8 -> 5 -> 7 -> 4 -> 8 etc
            and a 2nd snake around the even entries
            i.e. 6 -> 2 -> 3 -> 1 -> 6 etc
REORIENT    List of ALL input chans, in new positions they will have.
            e.g. for 4-chans: Data 4 1 2 3
            sends Ch1 to 4, Ch2 -> 1, Ch3 -> 2 and Ch4 -> 3
MIRRORPLANE Line around which frame is (symmetrically) mirrored.
            Values can be any (integer) outchannel number OR
            any half-way position between outchans e.g. 1.5, 2.5
            With N chans, 'N.5' lies between Nth & 1st chan.
-b (Mode 5) Convert bilateral to ring (Default, ring to bilateral).
            Numbering of outchans can be ring, or bilateral.
            e.g. for 7 outchans ...
                 RING                   BILATERAL
                   1                        1
                7     2                  2     3
               6       3                4       5
                5     4                  6     7
            All multichan pan programs assume RING numbering.
            Use this mode to convert out of and into bilateral format.
-b (Mode 8) Convert BEAST bilateral to ring (Default, ring to BEAST).
            BEAST bilateral numbering for 8 outchans ...
                 RING                     BEAST
                   1                        7
                8     2                  1     2
               7       3                3       4
                6     4                  5     6
                   5                        8
SWAPA,SWAPB The 2 channels that are to be swapped.
CHANINFO    A single channel number, or a list of channels in a file.
GAIN        Gain to apply to enveloped channels (can vary over time).

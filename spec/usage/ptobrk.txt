USAGE:     ptobrk withzeros binary-pitchfile outtextfile min-pitch-dur

Converts binary pitch data to time-frq pairs in a breakpoint textfile.

This process should be used instead of 'repitch pchtotext'
for files containing no-pitch and no-sound markers.

MIN-PITCH-DUR    gives minimum time (in milliseconds) any stretch of pitchdata
                 must persist to be regarded as valid data.
                 (range 1 ms to 1000 ms: suggest 20 ms).

USAGE:
ceracu ceracu inf outf cyclcnts mincycdur chans outdur echo echshift [-o] [-l]

Repeat src in several cyclestreams that resynchronise after specified counts.
One complete pass is a "resync-cycle". e.g. for 10 12 15
Source repeat 10,12 and 15 times before cyclestreams resynchronise ....
IF source sound is "A", cyclestreams would be ....
A     A     A     A     A     A     A     A     A     A     A (10 times)
A    A    A    A    A    A    A    A    A    A    A    A    A (12 times)
A   A   A   A   A   A   A   A   A   A   A   A   A   A   A   A (15 times)
|--------------------------Resync cycle --------------------|
|---| = Mincycdur (see below)

CYCLECNTS A list of integers, being
          the number of repeats in each cyclestream before the streams resync.
MINCYCDUR Time before the first repeat in the fastest cyclestream.
          If set to ZERO, assumed to be the duration of the input sound.
CHANS:    Number of channels in output. (Not ness same as number of cyclestreams)
OUTDUR    Duration of output. (If set to ZERO, outputs a single resync-cycle).
          Process always outputs a WHOLE NUMBER of complete resync-cycles,
          equal to or greater than the specified output duration.
          If true duration > 1 hour, sound curtailed, unless "-o" flag set.
ECHO      Single-echo-delay of entire output, in secs (Set to zero for no echo).
ECHSHIFT  Spatial offset of echo-delay (an integer value). (Ignored if no echo)
          1 = 1 chan to right; 2 = 2 chans to right; -1 = 1 channel to left; etc
-o        Override duration restriction, to produce all resync-cycles (CARE!).
-l        Output channels arranged linearly (default: arranged in a circle).

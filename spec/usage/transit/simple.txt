USAGE: transit simple 1-5 infile outfile
       focus dur steps max dec [-tthres -dlim -etlim -mmaxdur] [-l]

Place repetitions of a mono sound on tangent path to & from 8-channel array.
Lspkrs equidistant in a ring, numbered clockwise, Output is a mixfile.

MODES:
      1                2                3               4               5
   (glancing)      (edgewise)      (crossing)         (close)        (central)
->>---O->>      ->>--O---O->>           O             O   O             O
  O       O                    ->>--O-------O->>                    O       O
                  O         O                  ->>-O---------O->>
 O         O                       O         O                 ->>-O---------O->>
                  O         O                      O         O
  O       O                         O       O                       O       O
      O              O   O              O             O   O             O

FOCUS  Centre of motion. Integer for odd modes, 1.5 ; 2.5 etc for even modes.
DUR    Duration of motion from edge to centre (ONLY).
STEPS  Count of events from edge to centre (ONLY).
MAXA   (Modes 1-4) Maxangle from centreline reached( < 90).
MAXD   (Mode 5) Max distance from centre reached, where halfradius = 1.
DEC    Gain decrement (>0 <1) on passing from one event to the next.

To extend motion further (increasing duration and total number of events)....
gain decrement can be modofied with these 4 parameters ... 

THRESH Threshold (overall) gain at which gain decrement starts to increase.
LIM    Maximum level of gain decrement after this point (> DEC).
TLIM   Minimum (overall) gain at which event ends ( < THRESH).
MAXDUR Max duration motion edge-to-centre (in case TLIM never reached) ( >= DUR).

-l     Motion towards left of focal position (Default: motion towards right).

Converts frqequency-brkpnt and peakdata textfiles to a standard midi data file.

USAGE:
convert_to_midi  frqbrkpnt  peakvals  datalen  staccato  outfilename  system

FRQBRKPNT   File of time-frq pairs for note starts and ends.
PEAKVALS    Levels of each note (Range 0-1).
DATALEN     Number of peaks (no. of vals in 'frqbrkpnt' should be datalen * 4)
STACCATO    Force staccato output.
OUTFILENAME (File extension will be forced to '.mid' or '.rmi')
SYSTEM      0 gives Standard Midi File, with extension '.mid'
            1 gives PC Midi File, with extension '.rmi'

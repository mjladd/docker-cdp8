USAGE: tangent list
 1 textfile  outf dur maxangle dec [-ffoc] [-jjitter] [-sslow] [-r] [-l]
 2 textfile  outf dur skew     dec [-ffoc] [-jjitter]          [-r] [-l]

Play sequence of mono sounds (listed in the textfile) on tangent path to (or from)
8-channel front-centred lspkr array, numbered clockwise, from "1" at front-centre.

MODE 1  For focus at 1, tangent starts along line at right-angles to lspkr 2.
MODE 2  For focus at 1 tangent starts along line formed by lspkrs 2 and 3.

DUR        Duration of output file.
MAXANGLE  (Mode 1) Motion between 3 lspkr pairs (e.g. 1-2, 2-3, 3-4)
           Max angle of rotation of motion lies between 3rd pair (  90 to 135 ).
          (lsprks 3-4 in the example)
SKEW      (Mode 2) Motion between 3 lspkr pairs (e.g. 1-2, 2-3, 3-4)
           Skew is ratio (0-1) of time spent between last pair & penultimate pair
          (lspkr pairs 3-4 and 2-3, in the example)
DEC        Loudness decimation on passing from one event to the next.
FOC        Focal lpskr to or from which motion takes place (default, front centre).
JITTER     Randomisation of event timings (0-1, default 0) Can vary over time.
SLOW       Slow (or speed up) the pan-motion acceleration (default 0.5).
           Smaller values make stream arrive more slowly at goal.
           For approaching motion, 0.5 makes arrival not too fast.
           For receding motion, 1.0, makes initial motion rapid.
-r         Sounds RECEDE (default, sound approaches)
-l         Motion to or from LEFT of focal lspkr (default: to or from right).

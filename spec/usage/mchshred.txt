USAGE:
mchshred shred 1 infile outfile repeats chunklen scatter outchans 
mchshred shred 2 infile outfile repeats chunklen scatter

Sound is cut into random segments, which are then reassembled
in random order, within the original duration.
Further shreds, shred the previously shredded output.

The shredding process distributes the output segments over
 a randopm permutation of the output channels.

REPEATS   no. of repeats of shredding process.
CHUNKLEN  average length of chunks to cut & permute.
SCATTER   randomisation of cuts (0 to K): default 1.
          where K = total no. of chunks (snd-duration/chunklen).
          If scatter = 0, reorders without shredding.
OUTCHAN   Mode 1 shreds a mono input to a multichannel output.
          with 'outchan' channels.
          Mode 2 shreds an already multichannel file.

FIND GRAINS IN A SOUND, AND EXTEND AREA THAT CONTAINS THEM

USAGE:
grainex extend inf outf wsiz trof plus stt end

WSIZ    sizeof window in ms, determines size of grains to find.
TROF    acceptable trough height, relative to adjacent peaks (range >0 - <1)
PLUS    how much duration to add to source.
STT     Time of start of grain material within source.
END     Time of end of grain material within source.

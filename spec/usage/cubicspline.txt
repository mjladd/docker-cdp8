USAGE: cubicspline datafile outdatafile pointcnt srate [-s]

Generate smoothed curve from input data re spectrum.

DATAFILE     textfile of frq/amp pairs.
OUTDATAFILE  textfile of smoothed data.
POINTCNT     no of analysis points required in handling data.
SRATE        samplerate of eventual sound output.
-s           if spectral amp falls to zero before the nyquist,
             keep zeroed end of spectrum at zero.
.

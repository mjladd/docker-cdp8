USAGE:
tweet tweet 1 infile outfile exclude pitchdata minlevel pkcnt chirp [-w]
tweet tweet 2 infile outfile exclude pitchdata minlevel frq chirp [-w]
tweet tweet 3 infile outfile exclude pitchdata minlevel [-w]

Replace FOFs in vocal sound by synthetic tweets or (mode 3) noise.

PITCHDATA   A breakpoint file with time and frq information.
            (Use "repitch getpitch 2").
            May contain zeros (indicating moments of no-signal)
            but it must contain SOME significant frequency information.
EXCLUDE     Areas in src from which FOFs will NOT be extracted.
            If not wanted, enter "0". Otherwise pairs of times in textfile.
MINLEVEL    A level in dBs below level of loudest FOF found,
            FOFs below this level rejected. NB Zero = NO FOFS rejected.
PKCNT       Number of peaks in impulse. (1-200) (frq changes with FOF size)
FRQ         Freq of peaks in impulse.   (1-200) (frq fixed).
CHIRP       Glissing of impulse. (Range 0 to 30) (Care, output may clip).

-w    FOFs are windowed (cosine smooth of edges)

strange glis 1 infile outfile -fN|-pN [-i] glisrate        [-ttopfrq] 
strange glis 2 infile outfile -fN|-pN [-i] glisrate hzstep [-ttopfrq] 
strange glis 3 infile outfile -fN|-pN [-i] glisrate        [-ttopfrq] 

CREATE GLISSANDI INSIDE THE (CHANGING) SPECTRAL ENVELOPE OF ORIGINAL SOUND

MODES :-
1   shepard tones.
2   inharmonic glide.
3   self-glissando.

-f     extract formant envelope linear frqwise,
       using 1 point for every N equally-spaced frequency-channels.
-p     extract formant envelope linear pitchwise,
       using N equally-spaced pitch-bands per octave.
-i     quicksearch for formants (less accurate).

glisrate rate of glissing in semitones per second (-ve val for downward gliss).
hzstep   partials-spacing in inharmonic glide.
         Range: FROM channel-frq-width TO nyquist/2.
topfrq   top of spectrum: must be > 2*chanwidth of analysis (default:nyquist)

glisrate may vary over time.

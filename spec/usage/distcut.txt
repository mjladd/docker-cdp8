USAGE:
distcut distcut 1 infile generic_outfilename cyclecnt exp [-climit]
OR:
distcut distcut 2 infile generic_outfilename cyclecnt cyclestep exp [-climit]

Cut sound into elements with falling envelope.

CYCLECNT  number of wavesets in each outfile.
CYCLESTEP number of wavesets steps from start of one group to start of next.
          (In mode 1 : cyclestep = cyclecnt : waveset-groups abutted and disjunct).
EXP       Envelope Decay shape. 1 linear : >1 more rapid decay : < 1 less rapid.
LIMIT     Minimum level of output events to accept (value 70 = -70dB).

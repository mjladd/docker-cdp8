USAGE:
phasor phasor infile outfile streams phasfrq shift ochans [-ooffset] [-s] [-e]

Introduce phasing into signal : Takes a MONO input file.

STREAMS  Number of output streams that phase-interact (Range 2 - 8)
PHASFRQ  Frequency of packets (phase shifts forward then back in a single packet).
SHIFT    Maximum phaseshift with packet: (Range 0 - 12 semitones).
OCHANS   Number of output channels (not greater than number of streams).
OFFSET   The streams may be time-offset from one another..
        "Offset" is time-offset of the most-offset stream (Range 0 - 500 mS).
         The other streams are offset by intermediate amounts.

-s       Output chans (lspkrs) encircle audience : only with more than 2 ochans.
-e       Prints warnings re rounding-errors in calculating time-shifted streams.

"shift" and "phasfrq" can vary through time,
but extreme phasfrq changes or reversals will produce anomalous output.

USAGE:
topantail2 topantail infile outfile startgate endgate [-ssplicelen] [-bbacktrack]

STARTGATE  level at start before which sound is to be removed (0-1).
ENDGATE    level at end after which sound is to be removed (0-1).
SPLICELEN  splicelen in mS.
BACKTRACK  backtrack from initial gate point to an earlier splice point, in mS.

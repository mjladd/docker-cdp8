USAGE:
envnu peakchop 1 infil outfil wsize pkwidth risetime tempo gain
              [-ggate -qskew -sscatter -norm -rrepeat -mmiss]
OR:
envnu peakchop 2 infil outfil wsize pkwidth risetime [-ggate -qskew]

Isolate peaks in source, and (Mode 1) play back at specified tempo,
or (Mode 2) output peak-isolating envelope.

WSIZE    windowsize (in mS) for extracting envelope (1-64 dflt 50)
PKWIDTH  width of retained peaks (in mS) (dflt 20mS).
         (Must be less than minimum distance between data peaks).
RISETIME risetime from zero to peak (in mS) (dflt 10mS)
TEMPO    tempo of resulting output, MM = events per minute.
GAIN     lower this if rapid tempo causes peaks to overlap (0-1, dflt 1)
GATE     level (relative to max) below which peaks ignored (0-1 dflt 0)
SKEW     envelope centring on peak (0 to 1: dflt 0.25). 0.5 = centred,
         0 = peak at envelope start,  1 = peak at envelope end.
SCATTER  randomisation of output times (0-1 dflt 0)
NORM     force peakevent levels towards that of loudest (0-1 dflt 0)
REPEAT   number of times peakevents are repeated (0-256 dflt 0)
MISS     use peakevent, skip next 'miss' peakevents (0-64 default 0).


All parameters may vary in time, EXCEPT wsize, gate and skew.

Times in breakpoint files are times in the output.

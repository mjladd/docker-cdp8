TREMOLO A SOUND, WIDTH NARROWED, AFTER PEAK

USAGE: tremenv tremenv infile outfile frq depth winsize fineness

FRQ          frequency of tremenv (0 - 500) 
DEPTH        depth of tremenv: Range(0 to 1: default 1)
WINSIZE      Size of window for envelope extraction (mS) (Range 1 - 40)
FINENESS     Squeeze width of tremenv (integer value >= 1).

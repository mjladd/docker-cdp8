Use spectral features data to reassemble MONO source file.

USAGE:
fturanal synth 1-10 inwavfil outfile infeaturefile [-ssplicelen]

INFEATUREFILE list of feature values obtained from the analysis file
          derived from the INWAVFIL.
        (a) Segment start-times (+ duration of source).
        (b) (Median) Frequency of 1st formant.
        (c) (Median) Frequency of 2nd formant.
        (d) (Median) Frequency of 3rd formant.
        (e) Spectral brightness (Range 0 to 1).

SPLICELEN for cutting segments, in mS (Range 2 to 15: default 5).

MODE 1  Reassemble in order of increasing 1st Formant.
MODE 2  Reassemble in order of decreasing 1st Formant.
MODE 3  Reassemble in order of increasing 2nd Formant.
MODE 4  Reassemble in order of decreasing 2nd Formant.
MODE 5  Reassemble in order of increasing 3rd Formant.
MODE 6  Reassemble in order of decreasing 3rd Formant.
MODE 7  Reassemble in order of increasing Brightness.
MODE 9  Reassemble in order of increasing Formants, summed.
MODE 10 Reassemble in order of decreasing Formants, summed.

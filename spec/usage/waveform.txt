USAGE: waveform make 1 infile outfile time cnt.
OR:    waveform make 2 infile outfile time dur.
OR:    waveform make 3 infile outfile time dur balance.

Generate a wavetable from existing sound.

Input file must be MONO.

Mode 1 extracts a group of half-wavesets at specified time in infile.
Mode 2 extracts given duration of source, cutting at zero-crossing.
Mode 3 combines specified duration-segment of infile with a sinusoid.

CNT     Number of half-wavesets to extract.
DUR     Duration, in mS, of sound-clip to extract.
TIME    Time in infile where data is extracted.
BALANCE Level of extracted material relative to imposed sinusoid.

In each case, the cut segment is abutted to its inversion,
making a symmetric wavecycle (and doubling its duration).
In Modes 1 & 2 this forms the output.
In Mode 3 this is superimposed on a sinusoid of wavelength DUR * 2.

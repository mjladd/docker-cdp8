USAGE:
multiosc multiosc 1 outfile dur frq1 frq2 amp2 srate dovesplice
multiosc multiosc 2 outfile dur frq1 frq2 amp2 frq3 amp3 srate dovesplice
multiosc multiosc 3 outfile dur frq1 frq2 amp2 frq3 amp3 frq4 amp4 srate dovesplice


MODE 1 Oscillation of oscillation.
MODE 2 Oscillation of oscillation of oscillation.
MODE 3 Oscillation of oscillation of oscillation of oscillation.

DUR        Duration of synthesized sound, or textfile output.
FRQ1       (Possibly time-varying) driving frequency of output.
FRQ2 etc   Nested (possibly time-varying) oscillations of table read.
AMP2 etc   Amplitude of suboscillations (Range 0 - 1).
SRATE      Sample rate of synthesized sound.
DOVESPLICE Length of splice of start and end of output sound (mS).

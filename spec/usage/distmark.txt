USAGE:
distmark distmark 1 infile outfile marklist unitlen [-ststretch] [-rrand] [-f] [-t]
distmark distmark 2 infile outfile marklist unitlen [-ststretch] [-rrand] [-f]

Interpolate between waveset-groups at marked points in MONO soundfile.
In Mode 2 interpolate within ALTERNATE marked blocks.

MARKLIST List of times within source at which to find waveset-groups.
UNITLEN  (approx) size of waveset group to find (mS). Can vary over time.
         Min UNITLEN < 1/2 of min step between Times in MARKLIST.
TSTRETCH Timestretch distances between marks, in making output.
RAND     Randomise duration of interpolated wavesets (Range 0 - 1).
         Randomisation decreases waveset lengths (heard "pitch" higher).
-f   Flip phase of alternate wavesets.
-t   Add original (remaining) tail of source sound to output.

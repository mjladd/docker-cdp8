newsynth synthesis 1 outf spectrum srate dur frq

    [-urise] [-dfall] [-fsteady] [-ssplice] [-nN] [-a] [-z] [-x]
    [-tspacetype] [-rrotspeed]
    [[-m] [-j] [efrom -Etime] [cto -Ctime]]
newsynth synthesis 4 outf srate dur frq atk ea dec ed atoh gtow [-fflv] [-rrnd|-e]
newsynth synthesis 5 outf srate dur frq damping k b

Synthesize complex spectra.

MODE 1 Generates tones with any number of (possibly varying) partials.
MODE 2 Generates wave-packet streams with any number of (possibly varying) partials.
MODE 3 Multichan Mode1 where partials (spread over N octaves) fade in-out randomly.
MODE 4 Fractally arrayed spikes.
MODE 5 Duffing damped oscillator (frq, amplitude and damping can vary through time).

Hit key '1' '2 '3' '4' or '5 to see more on individual modes, or 'e' to exit.

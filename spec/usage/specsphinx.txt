USAGE: specsphinx specsphinx 1 analfile1 analfile2 outanalfile
       [-aampbalance] [-ffrqbalance]

IMPOSE CHANNEL AMPLITUDES OF FILE2 ON CHANNEL FRQS OF FILE 1

AMPBALANCE  Proportion of File1 amps retained (Default 0)
FRQBALANCE  Proportion of File2 frqs injected into output spectrum
(Default 0)

USAGE: specsphinx specsphinx 2 analfile1 analfile2 outanalfile
       [-bbias] [-ggain]

MULTIPLY THE SPECTRA

BIAS    When non-zero, adds proportion of original files to output.

      < 1 : add analfile1 to outanalfile in proportion (-bias)/(1+bias)
      > 1 : add analfile2 to outanalfile in proportion (bias/(1-bias)
GAIN   Overall gain on output.

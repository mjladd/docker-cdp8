USAGE: flutter flutter inf outf chanseq freq depth gain [-r]

Distribute a loudness tremulation around a multichannel output
CHANSEQ  is a textfile containing a list of "chansets".
         A "chanset" is a list of any of the channels in the input file.
         Level of every chan in 1st chanset varies for 1 cycle of flutter frq,
                 then level of every chan in 2nd chanset varies for next cycle, etc.
         around the sequence of chansets.
         When end of chansets list is reached, begins again at start.
         (but see '-r' flag).
FREQ     Frequency (in Hz) of the loudness variation (tremulation).
DEPTH    Depth (0-16) of the loudness variation.
         As values approach 1, loudness troughs approach zero.
         Values > 1 produce narrower peaks in the tremulation.
GAIN     Overall gain (0-1) of the loudness variation.

Both Freq and Depth may vary over time.

-r       Randomisation of chanset order.
         Once all the chansets have been used,
         their order is randomly permuted before they are used again,
         and once all have been used a 2nd time,
         their order is permuted again, and so on.

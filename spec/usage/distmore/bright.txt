USAGE:  distmore bright 1-3 infil outfil marklist [-ssplicelen -d]


Reorders sound segments in order of average zero-crossing rate
.
MARKLIST A list of timemarks in source, marking (paired) Heads and Tails.
         e.g. consonant onset, and vowel continuation of source.
         (It is assumed that the first mark is at a Head segment.)

Extract data ...
MODE 1   from Heads, & from Tails cut to segments, size approx equal to Heads.
MODE 2   from Heads and Tails, as defined by marklist.
MODE 3   from Tails only.

SPLICELEN Length of splice in mS
-d        Output in decreasing order of brightness. (Default: increasing).

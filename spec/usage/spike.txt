USAGE:
spike spike infile outfile peak(s) upslope downslope [-mmaxup] [-dmaxdown] [-n]

Envelope the input sound to spike at the peak.

PEAKS(S)  Is either a single time (single peak) or a list of trof-peak-trof triplets,
          Adjacent trofs may be at the same time, but other times must advance.

UPSLOPE   Power-value for slope of envelope before peak(s) Range (1 to 100).
DOWNSLOPE Power-value for slope of envelope after peak(s) Range (1 to 100).

MAXUP     Maximum duration of upward approach: Range (0 to 1 secs).
MAXDOWN   Maximum duration of downward approach: Range (0 to 1 secs).
          (In both cases ZERO implies NO maximum)

-n        Raise all peaks to that of maximum.

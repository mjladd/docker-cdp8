USAGE:
quirk quirk 1-2 infile outfile powfac

Distort signal by raising sample values to a power.

POWFAC  Range 0.01 to 100.
             < 1: exaggerates signal contour.
             > 1: smooths signal contour.

MODE 1  Applies power factor over amplitude range of individual half-wavesets.
MODE 2  Applies power factor over amplitude range of entire signal.

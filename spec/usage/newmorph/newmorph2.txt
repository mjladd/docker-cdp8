USAGE: newmorph newmorph2 1 analfileA outtextfile peakcnt

Find frequencies of most prominent spectral peaks (in order of prominence).
Ouput these as a textfile list, a "peaksfile".

OR:    newmorph newmorph2 2-3 analfileB outanalfile peaksfile 
           startmorph  endmorph  exponent  peakcnt  [-rrand]

Morph between dissimilar spectra
(Peaks of analfileB move towards peaks extracted from analfileA).

PEAKCNT    Number of most-prominent source-peaks to find.
PEAKSFILE  Textfile listing goal-peak frequencies: most prominent first.
STARTMORPH Time at which morph starts.
ENDMORPH   Time at which morph ends.
EXPONENT   Exponent of interpolation.
-r         Randomisation of the goal peak frequency. (Range 0 - 1)

MODES

2   Sound (gradually) tuned to harmonic field specified in textfile "peaksfile".
3   Ditto, but interpolation is timewise-cosinusoidal.

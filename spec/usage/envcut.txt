USAGE:
envcut envcut 1 infile generic_outfilename attack envlen exp [-climit]
OR:
envcut envcut 2 infile generic_outfilename attack envlen step exp [-climit]

Cut sound into elements with falling envelope.

ENVLEN    Duration of each outfile.
STEP      Timestep between start of each envelope cut.
          (In mode 1 : envlen = step : cut-segments abutted and disjunct).
ATTACK    Envelope attack duration (mS) : (must be less than half envelope duration).
EXP       Envelope Decay shape. 1 linear : >1 more rapid decay : < 1 less rapid.
LIMIT     Minimum level of output events to accept (value 70 = -70dB).

USAGE:
specnu rand inanalfile outanalfile [-ttimescale] [-ggrouping]

Randomise order of spectral windows.

TIMESCALE determines no of windows locally-randomised. (dflt all windows)
GROUPING  consecutive windows grouped into sets of size "grouping".
          These sets are randomised (default grouping = 1 window per set).

"Timescale" may vary over time

USAGE:
specnu remove 1-2 inanalfile outanalfile midimin midimax rangetop atten

MODE 1: removes pitch and its harmonics up to a specified frequency limit
MODE 2: removes everything else.

MIDIMIN    minimum pitch to remove (MIDI).
MIDIMAX    maximum pitch to remove (MIDI).
RANGETOP   frequency at which search for harmonics stops (Hz).
ATTEN      attenuation of suppressed components (1 = max, 0 = none).

Midi range normally should be small.
If range is octave or more, whole spectrum between lower pitch & rangetop
will be removed.

USAGE:
specnu clean sigfile noisfile outanalfile persist noisgain

Eliminate frqbands in 'sigfile' falling below maxlevels found in 'noisfile'

PERSIST    min-time chan-signal > noise-level, to be retained (Range 0-1000 ms).
NOISGAIN   multiplies noiselevels in noisfile channels before they are used
           for comparison with infile signal: (Range 1 - 40).

Both input files are analysis files.
'noisfil' is a sample of noise (only) from the 'sigfile'.

USAGE:
impulse impulse outfile dur pitch chirp slope pkcnt level [-ggap] [-ssrate] [-cchans]

Create a stream of impulses.

DUR    Duration of output sound.
       If set to zero, a single impulse is produced.
PITCH  Pitch of impulse-stream (MIDI). (Determines stream-frq)
CHIRP  Glissing of impulse. (Range 0 to 30) (Care, output may clip).
SLOPE  How quickly impulse rises and falls in level. (Range 1 to 20)
PKCNT  Number of peaks in impulse. (Range 1 - 200)
       'Pkcnt' divided by stream-frq -> (unglissed) frq of impulses.
LEVEL  Impulse level (Range 0-1).
GAP    Relative size of any silent gap between impulses.
       If > zero, lowers apparent pitch of stream.
       IF < zero, chirps overlap, raises apparent pitch of stream.
SRATE  Sample-rate of output file  (Default 44100).
CHANS  Number of channels in output file. (Default 1).

"PITCH","GLIS","SLOPE","CHIRP","LEVEL" and "GAP" can vary through time.

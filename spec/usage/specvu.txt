USAGE:
specvu specvu 1 inanalfile outtextfile windowno
specvu specvu 2 inanalfile outtextfile

MODE 1: Extracts the data in a specified spectral window as a textfile.
MODE 2: Finds (abs) min & max frq, and min & max amp of spectral data.
        and outputs to a textfile.

WINDOWNO Number of the window to extract
